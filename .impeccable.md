## Design Context

### Users
SVA graduate students and visitors browsing the Prototyping for Masters course and its student work.

### Brand Personality
Bold, editorial, experimental, and clear. The experience should feel confident and creatively rigorous without becoming difficult to navigate.

### Aesthetic Direction
A high-contrast, typography-led course site with an editorial composition, expressive scale, and restrained supporting details. Preserve the existing black-and-white foundation and playful palette switching.

### Design Principles
- Let typography carry the visual identity.
- Use a clear underlying grid so expressive scale still feels intentional.
- Keep interactions simple and immediately understandable.
- Preserve generous space and strong contrast.
- Adapt the composition cleanly across screen sizes.
